# Optional concept illustration

Use-case: scientific-educational illustration, not an evidence diagram.

Create one landscape 3:2 academic concept illustration on a pure white
background. Three small, friendly humanoid teaching-role figures stand near a
compact filing cabinet with open memory drawers and blank record cards. Give
the planner a clipboard and a blue accent, the teacher a small open book and
an orange accent, and the reviewer a magnifier or simple glasses and a green
accent. These are personified software roles, not three independently trained
models. Use gentle expressions and a careful, precise drawing style suited to
a printed technical report.

Keep approximately 70 percent of the canvas white and leave breathing room
between figures. Use thin charcoal and grey linework, mostly white objects,
and sparse palette A accents: blue #0072B2, orange #E69F00, green #009E73.
The cabinet and file cards should suggest saving and retrieving evidence;
they must have absolutely no labels. No text, letters, digits, equations,
metrics, checkmarks, logos or watermarks anywhere. No graph axes, arrows,
dashboard, dark background, gradients, shadows, 3D rendering or photorealism.
This is optional explanatory artwork only; it must not imply experimental
success, verified learning gains or real customer adoption.
