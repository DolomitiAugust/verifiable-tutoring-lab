# Historical case outcomes — Academic Figure Prompt, palette A

## Layer 1 — Global description

Create a compact 162 × 75 mm editable research case map titled **Four cases
against their frozen expectations**. Arrange four white modules in a two-by-two
grid, with each showing its original expected completion, observed completion,
and the mechanism explaining that outcome. This is a qualitative case map,
not a statistical success-rate chart. Preserve every historical label.

## Layer 2 — Section-by-section description

=== C03. ACROSS-INSTANCE MEMORY ===

Blue outline. Show **Partial → complete** and **Earlier error retrieved after
restart; new program facts checked.** This success is above the case's own
frozen expectation, not a comparison against another trained model.

=== C04. STUDENT ISOLATION ===

Green outline. Show **Partial → complete** and **New student: no other
learner's history; unanswered status preserved.** Do not suggest the system
discarded the record or that a model inferred mastery.

=== C08. CONSTRAINED FOLLOW-UP ===

Orange outline. Show **Complete → incomplete** and **Executable output, wrong
edit: accumulator changed instead of list.** Program execution alone could not
establish compliance with the requested edit.

=== S02. VALID EMPTY PREDICTION ===

Orange outline. Show **Complete → incomplete** and **Correct empty output;
valid prediction recorded as unanswered.** This is the historical uncorrected
run. Do not use repaired regression output to change its label.

## Layer 3 — Global annotations and evidence binding

Retain two bottom notes: **Success mechanisms: tools/templates + memory, not
improved model collaboration** and **C07 remains an expected limitation; no
case was relabeled**. Bind expectations to original core/supplementary frozen
results and observed completeness to their retained criterion reviews. Check
that all criteria are scored complete for C03/C04 and that C08/S02 contain
failed or partial criteria. Verify final sources are tool/template rather than
adopted model prose. Keep Chinese and English nodes, states and direction
identical. Exact source paths and SHA-256 values belong in provenance.

## Layer 4 — Style specifications

At least 70% white, with thin rounded outlines and no shaded cards, badges,
gradients or decorative charts. Use palette A blue `#0072B2`, orange `#E69F00`,
green `#009E73`, charcoal `#263746`, and grey `#62727C`. Keep titles, state
arrows and case explanations visibly separated, using approximately 7.4–11.2 pt
searchable sans-serif text. Export editable SVG, embedded-font PDF and 300-dpi
PNG. Inspect bilingual wording, text collisions, borders, margins and greyscale.
